# v0.4.16 – EU AI Act Article 50 Compliance 🇪🇺

## ⚖️ Full EU AI Act Transparency Compliance

This release implements all Article 50 obligations for the RMAP Chatbot, ahead of the **2 August 2026** deadline. Two commits, two files changed — clean and auditable.

## What changed

### Art. 50(1) – AI Interaction Disclosure

Users must be informed they are interacting with an AI system.

**Implementation:** Dify `opening_statement` — displayed at chat start:

> "Hello, I am the RMaP Copilot. I am an AI system based on Large Language Models (LLMs). I assist with searching and summarising RMaP publications. Please always verify my answers against the cited original documents. (EU AI Act Art. 50(1))"

Combined with the existing 🤖 emoji icon and "RMAP Chatbot" name for defense-in-depth.

### Art. 50(2) – Machine-Readable Watermark

AI-generated content must be marked in a machine-readable format and detectable as artificially generated.

**Implementation:** `final_answer_sanitizer.py` appends to every non-empty answer:

```markdown
---
*Disclaimer (EU AI Act Art. 50): This text was synthetically generated by an AI system
(RMaP Copilot via Dify/Ollama). Please verify against cited sources.*
<!-- AI_GENERATED_CONTENT_RMAP -->
```

| Layer | Mechanism |
|---|---|
| **Human-readable** | Italic Markdown footer under `---` separator |
| **Machine-readable** | HTML comment `<!-- AI_GENERATED_CONTENT_RMAP -->` — invisible in rendered chat, present in raw API/SSE payloads |

### Bugfix: Opening Statement Survives Import

`import_dify_dsl.sh` was reading `features` from the wrong YAML path (`dsl.get("features")` → `dsl.get("app", {}).get("features")`). Every import silently reset `opening_statement` to `''`. Fixed.
